Landslide

FLEETWOOD MAC · 1975

The story behind the song

Written by Stevie Nicks during a period of uncertainty in her life, Landslide reflects on change, loss and the passing of time. The song was inspired by her feelings of being at a crossroads, unsure of what the future held, and the need to find strength in letting go.

Why it resonates

With its honest lyrics and gentle, introspective sound, Landslide captures the universal feeling of navigating life's transitions. It reminds us that even in moments of loss and uncertainty, there is beauty in moving forward.

Cat's in the Cradle

HARRY CHAPIN · 1974

The story behind the song

Inspired by a poem written by Sandy Chapin, Cat's in the Cradle tells the story of a father who is too busy to spend time with his son. As the years pass, their roles reverse, and the father realises that his son has grown into someone who is just as unavailable as he was.

Why it resonates

The song reminds listeners how quickly time passes and how easily relationships can be neglected. Its message about family, regret, and missed opportunities encourages us to appreciate the people in our lives while we still have the chance.

Evergreen

OMAR APOLLO · 2022

The story behind the song

Evergreen explores the emotional aftermath of a relationship where love was not returned in the same way. Through vulnerable lyrics and expressive vocals, Omar Apollo captures the pain of feeling inadequate and struggling to understand why someone could not love him as deeply as he loved them.

Why it resonates

The song reflects the insecurity and heartbreak that can come with unrequited love. Its emotional honesty connects with listeners who have questioned their worth after a relationship, reminding them that rejection does not determine their value.

Over Country

LIZZY MCALPINE · 2026

The story behind the song

Over Country explores the complicated emotions that remain after a relationship has changed. Through intimate storytelling and reflective lyrics, the song portrays the difficulty of moving forward while memories of the past continue to influence the present.

Why it resonates

The song captures the quiet uncertainty of letting go. Its gentle sound and emotional vulnerability reflect how healing is rarely straightforward, and how certain memories can stay with us even as life moves forward.

Every Breath You Take

THE POLICE · 1983

The story behind the song

Written by Sting during a difficult period in his personal life, Every Breath You Take explores jealousy, possessiveness, and the desire to control someone after a relationship has ended. Despite its gentle melody, the song carries a darker meaning than its romantic sound initially suggests.

Why it resonates

The contrast between its soothing melody and unsettling lyrics makes the song memorable. It encourages listeners to question the difference between genuine affection and obsession, showing how music can communicate complicated emotions.

The Scientist

COLDPLAY · 2002

The story behind the song

The Scientist explores the regret and confusion that can follow a relationship's breakdown. Through its simple piano melody and heartfelt lyrics, the song expresses a longing to return to the beginning, when things felt less complicated and mistakes had not yet been made.

Why it resonates

The song reflects the universal desire to undo mistakes and repair relationships. Its emotional simplicity captures the difficulty of accepting the past and moving forward when we wish we could start again.
